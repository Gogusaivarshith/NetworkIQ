# 🚀 NetworkIQ – Multi-Agent AI Supply Chain Optimization Platform

NetworkIQ is an AI-powered supply chain optimization platform that helps organizations identify inventory shortages, detect excess stock, and recommend profitable inventory transfers across warehouses using a multi-agent architecture.

Built for hackathons and enterprise-scale supply chain decision support.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Supply chains often suffer from:

- Overstock in one warehouse
- Shortages in another warehouse
- High transfer costs
- Stockout risks
- Slow manual decision making

NetworkIQ automates this process using AI-driven recommendations.
